Why should I hire students instead of an established painting company?
+
Because they can — and because of who they are.

Painting requires patience, diligence, attention to detail, and hard work. It sits in a sweet spot between skilled labor that requires heavy machinery and desk work that teaches people nothing. It's the perfect craft for someone driven, trainable, and hungry to prove themselves.

We don't just hire any student. We rigorously select Branch Managers who have the character, the work ethic, and the competitive drive to do this at a high level. Then we train them early, often, and continuously — on both the technical craft and the business side.

We'll be honest: we think you'll come away with a new appreciation for the next generation when you work with your Branch Manager. They will surprise you.
Why not just hire a "real" professional painting company?
+
We are professionals.

Collectively, VRSTY's leadership has been responsible for over 100,000 painting projects across 25 states and 2 countries. Our Branch Managers are trained, accountable, and backed by a company that has seen every situation imaginable.

Think of it like college football. Would you watch a college game? Of course — because those players are talented, coachable, and competitive, even if they haven't gone pro yet. Our Branch Managers are the same: elite-level recruits who are being developed into the professionals of tomorrow.

And frankly — we'd bet you've hired a "professional" contractor before and been disappointed. We aim to flip that entirely. Built Different.
How does your pricing compare to other companies?
+
If you got ten quotes from ten different painting companies, VRSTY would land in the middle. We're not the cheapest — because we carry real insurance, full workers' compensation coverage, professional-grade equipment, and the best materials available in the market.

We're also not the most expensive — because we don't have the overhead of a large corporate operation or the inflated pricing of so-called "exclusive" contractors.

The best way to understand our pricing is to get an estimate. It's free, it's on the spot, and there's no pressure or commitment required. You'll see exactly what you're getting for every dollar.
Do you guarantee your work?
+
Absolutely.

Here's how we like to frame it: you're hiring a local, student-led branch — but you have the full weight of VRSTY's leadership, systems, and standards behind every job. You're not on your own if something isn't right.

Every project comes with a VRSTY workmanship warranty. If something doesn't meet our standard after the job is complete, we'll come back and make it right. No runaround, no disappearing act — that's not who we are.

You can register your warranty online after the job is complete.
