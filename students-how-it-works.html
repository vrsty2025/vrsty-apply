In college athletics, the roster is the training ground for the pros. Every rep, every practice, every game builds something bigger than the scoreboard — it builds professionals.

Most people who competed at a high level — in sports, in student government, in theater, in debate, in any arena where the standard was high and the pressure was real — don't get to keep competing in college. That doesn't mean the drive disappears. It means it has nowhere to go.

VRSTY is built to change that. It's the training ground for the next generation of entrepreneurs, executives, engineers, and leaders — built on the same structure, discipline, and competitive culture that shaped you, whatever that arena looked like.

Your spring is pre-season. Your summer is your season. And how you perform is entirely up to you.

Your Journey

From Application to Launch

Here's the path from the day you apply to the day you're running your first crew.

01
Apply Online

Fill out the Branch Manager application. We're looking for driven people — former athletes, student leaders, anyone wired to outwork the room. 3.0+ GPA and a car on campus required. Takes about 5 minutes.

5 minutes
02
Interview & Selection

If you qualify, we'll reach out within 48 hours to schedule an interview. This is a selective program. We're not looking for everyone — we're looking for the right people.

Within 48 hours
03
Training Program

Accepted Branch Managers go through full VRSTY training before launch — sales, operations, crew management, customer service, and financials. You'll know exactly what you're doing before your first job.

Full training included
04
Spring Pre-Season

Before summer hits, you're already building. Spring is part-time — weekends, free hours — focused on sales, marketing, and territory development. You're lining up customers before your crew ever picks up a brush. Pre-season determines how big your season is.

Part-time · Spring semester
05
Launch Your Market

Summer is full-time. You've already built the pipeline in the spring — now you execute. Hire your crew, run your jobs, manage quality, and get paid based on what you produce. Your territory, your results.

Full-time · Summer
05
Grow & Advance

Top performing Branch Managers earn paths to Senior BM and District Manager roles — managing multiple markets and developing other students. The ceiling is yours to set.

Unlimited upside
