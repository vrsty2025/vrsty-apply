Who We're Looking For
The VRSTY Standard
  • Current college student in good standing
  • Strong academic record and proven work ethic
  • Varsity athlete, leader, or proven self-starter
  • Available for a full summer commitment
  • Reliable car on or near campus
  • Hungry to build something real, not just earn a paycheck
What Happens Next
  • We review your application within 24–48 hours
  • Qualified candidates are contacted for an intro meeting
  • If there's mutual interest, we move to 1–2 interviews
  • Accepted BMs receive full training before launch
  • You get assigned a territory and go to work
